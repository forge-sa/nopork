# No Pork Mod
- The target audience is muslim minecraft player
- The mod applies Nausea, Poison, and Weakness effects on player every time they eat raw/cooked pork chop
- Effects last 10 seconds (200 ticks)
- Also 1/10 chance of being thunderstruck
- And 1/20 chance of being teleported to the nether 
- The mod is not intended to hurt anybody's religious feelings
- Fabric 1.19.x-1.21.x
